       <i class="mono" style="--c:#2E7D32">SO</i>SEO Optimizer

       <i class="tp-logo"><img src="assets/logos/seoptimer.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></i>SEO Optimizer

Note on trademarks: these files are logos owned by their respective companies.
Showing a product's logo to identify a tool taught on a course is normal
nominative use, but each brand has its own guidelines on spacing, colour and
minimum size. Check those before using the marks in print or paid advertising.
